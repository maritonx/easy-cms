Easy CMS 1.0: the first version meant for production use. From 1.0, breaking changes come only in a major release, with an upgrade guide; renamed APIs keep working until the next major, with a deprecation warning. `@easy-cms/plugin-ecommerce`, `@easy-cms/plugin-graphql` and `@easy-cms/plugin-mcp` stay experimental. Upgrading from 0.x: https://easy-cms-website.vercel.app/docs/upgrading#to-1-0
