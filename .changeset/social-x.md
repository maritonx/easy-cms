An X channel (#380): connect your X account with your own X app's keys (OAuth 1.0a, keys that don't expire) and post the text with its link. The admin's counter and Write with AI count X's way (a link 23, an emoji 2), and the panel shows what the post costs on X's pay-per-use API before posting. The built-in channels (`facebook`, `line`, `x`) are exported for sites that set `channels` themselves.
